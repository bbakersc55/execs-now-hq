# Incident — a prospect received six broken questions, 2026-09-22

Brett Murray was emailed the pre-call questions at 17:25 on 22 September. Under
the heading *"Rate each one from 1 to 10 — 1 means it barely works today, 10
means it could not be better"* sat six open-ended essay questions. He answered
them in prose, which is the only sensible thing to do with what he was sent.

## What actually happened

Three failures in a row, each of which would have stopped it on its own.

1. **Prep suggested essay rewordings for the six `rating_1_10` questions.**
   Nothing told the model that those six are a one-line lead-in to a number
   rather than a question to answer, and nothing checked its answer.
2. **The template editor accepted them.** It showed a box of wording per
   question and said nothing about what kind of question each one was, so
   applying a rewording to a rating looked exactly like applying one to a
   written answer.
3. **The send panel never showed the body.** It showed the opening line the
   fractional had written and a sentence describing what would be added
   underneath — so the six broken questions were never on anybody's screen
   before they were on Brett's.

The first two are the same missing rule: **a rewording changes the words, never
the shape.** The third is the one that let it out of the building.

## What changed

**A rewording may not change a question's shape.** `apps/strategy/rewording.py`
holds the rule once, and both places that can reword are held to it: prep drops
a suggestion that fails and says which one it dropped, and the template editor
refuses it with the reason. For a rating the rule is that the component's name
— Vision, People, Data, Issues, Process, Traction — stays in the wording, the
wording stays a lead-in of a line, and it does not become an open question. The
editor now shows each question's shape beside the box that rewords it.

**Nothing sends from a panel that has not shown the body.** The panel renders
the whole email from the same builder the send uses, so it cannot show one thing
and post another, and the send button is disabled until it has. The form link
and the covering email the strategy map travels in got the same treatment; the
link in a preview is a placeholder, because a preview that minted a token would
leave a live credential behind every time somebody looked.

**The six are back to the seed's wording**, restored by
`manage.py repair_rating_prompts` — a one-off repair run by a person, with a dry
run first and an audit event, rather than a migration: migration history here
stays additive, and a data rewrite is something the owner asks for out loud.

**Brett's session keeps the broken wording**, because a session renders from the
template it froze and that is the whole point of the snapshot. It now carries a
**fractional-only note** saying what happened and what to do on the call, and
**a rating will save without its number** — his prose goes in beside each
component as the fractional's own note, and the ratings are taken on the call.
An unrated component stays unanswered: it is not a zero, it does not enter the
average, and it does not let the six report a lowest score.

## The audit: every send, and whether a person sees it first

| Producer | Who sends it | Body shown before it goes |
|---|---|---|
| `precall_questions` | FF/CF, from the session | **Fixed today** — was the intro only |
| `precall_invite` | FF/CF/VA, from the session | **Fixed today** — was nothing |
| `strategy_pdf` | FF/CF, from the session | **Fixed today** — the document had a true preview since AC-4.10; the email it travels in had none |
| `digest` | approved on the digest screen | Yes — the whole digest, per stakeholder |
| `manual` | composed in the Outbox | Yes — the person wrote it |
| `stage_rule` | drafted to the Outbox | Yes — Review & edit |
| `referral_touch` · `referral_onboarding` | drafted to the Outbox | Yes — Review & edit |
| `magic_link` · `note_pin_reset` | a person clicks, template-only | **No, and not fixed** — see below |
| `client_activity` · `precall_complete` · `cadence_change` | automatic, nobody presses send | No panel exists to show one in |
| `inbound_forward` | Module 6, not built | — |

**The two that are not fixed, and why it is a judgement rather than an
oversight.** A magic link and a PIN reset are template-only, carry nothing a
person wrote, and are the same words every time; the thing that varies is a
credential that a preview must not show. A panel there would be a page of
unchanging text with the important part starred out, which is the kind of
preview people learn to click past. **If the owner wants them previewed anyway,
it is an afternoon** — the builders are already separate from the senders.
