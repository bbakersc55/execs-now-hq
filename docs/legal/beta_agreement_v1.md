# Execs NOW HQ Beta Agreement

Execs NOW HQ is provided by Noble Rose LLC, doing business as Executives Now and Execs NOW HQ ("we"). It is in beta. By using it you agree to the following:

1. Work in progress. Features will change, some may be removed, and you may find bugs. Tell us when you do; that is the point of the beta.

2. Your data is yours, and private from us. We cannot see inside your practice: not your contacts, your clients' work, your notes, your email, or your meetings. We see only what you send through the Feedback button, and the usage totals (storage, AI spend, activity counts) needed to run the service. Whoever operates the servers can technically read the database and its backups; we do not, and we restrict that access to what is needed to run and back up the service.

3. Costs. During the beta we charge you only our direct cost of hosting and database for your practice, with no markup, invoiced monthly. Your email account and your AI console are set up under your own name and paid with your own card; their charges are yours and the app shows you what it spends. You agree to pay our at-cost invoices within 15 days. If an invoice goes unpaid for 30 days we may suspend your practice until it is paid.

4. Backups and data loss. We back up the service nightly. You agree to follow the backup and export guidance we provide and to keep your own exports of anything you cannot afford to lose. You agree to hold Noble Rose LLC harmless for any loss of data that results from not following that guidance.

5. Our software stays ours. You will not copy, reproduce, reverse-engineer, or replicate the design, workflows, templates, or concepts of Execs NOW HQ, in whole or in part, to build or help build a competing product, during the beta or after it. Ideas you share through Feedback may be used to improve the product without payment or attribution.

6. Access. You will not share access with anyone outside your practice, and you will not use the service for anything unlawful.

7. Leaving. The beta runs until we release the product or either of us ends it with 30 days' notice. Pricing for the released product will be shared before any charge beyond cost applies. On request we will export your data and delete your practice.
